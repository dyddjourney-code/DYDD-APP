# Companion Notes

## First Companion Use

The first agent should not try to become the whole school. It should serve one lesson at a time.

Inputs:

- Lesson title and content.
- Student's DesignID scores.
- Student's prior reflection answer.
- Chosen voice/persona.

Outputs:

- A short personalized reflection.
- A next-step question.
- Optional Scripture tie-in where appropriate.

## Future Personas

Possible guide modes:

- Shepherd: pastoral encouragement and care.
- Architect: structure, next steps, and systems.
- Teacher: biblical grounding and clarity.
- Companion: warm journey support.
