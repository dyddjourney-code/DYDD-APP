# DesignID Foundations

## Course Purpose

Help learners understand their God-given design and begin applying it to identity, relationships, work, service, and mission.

## Draft Modules

1. Welcome and orientation.
2. Understanding the DesignID framework.
3. Reading your personal design pattern.
4. Strengths, tensions, and growth edges.
5. Living your design in relationships.
6. Living your design in calling and work.
7. Next steps and guided reflection.
