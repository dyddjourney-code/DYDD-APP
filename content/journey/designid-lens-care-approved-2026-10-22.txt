UNPACK YOUR REFLECTIONS — CARE DRAFT
For Journey Module 3 / Section 3.2 / Lesson 2 — The DesignID Lens
October 22, 2026. Proposed copy for John’s review; not implemented.

Connect
Look at your Snapshot as a mirror, not a box. Notice what feels familiar and what surprises you.
Which part of your results best describes how you naturally respond, connect, or contribute? Share one everyday example.

Act
Choose one strength reflected in your results and look for an opportunity to use it intentionally this week.
Where will you practice this strength, whom could it serve, and what will you watch for as you use it?

Reflect
Think back to a specific situation or relationship that was difficult or misunderstood. Look with curiosity and grace, not regret.
What does your design help you understand about your response then? Knowing this now, what might you have done differently?

Explore
Consider a current relationship or responsibility at home, work, church, or in your community.
How could understanding your design help you communicate, collaborate, or serve better there? What is one adjustment you could try?

Presentation: Full Connect, Act, Reflect, Explore headings only; no redundant individual letters. Snapshot remains the reference; no score-transcription exercise.
