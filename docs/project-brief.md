# DYDD Online School Project Brief

## Core Idea

Create an online school for Discover Your Divine Design where learners can move through biblical basics, evangelism, DesignID, DesignPD, spiritual gifts, and practical life-purpose training.

This is more than a course portal. The opportunity is to make the learning path personal. A student should eventually be able to hear the lesson through their own design language, such as Shepherd, Architect, Connector, Teacher, or other DYDD companion modes.

## Why This Fits

- DYDD already has assessment tools and interpretive content.
- John already has a DesignID class built inside GoHighLevel.
- Vercel and Supabase are strong enough for a custom school without being locked into an LMS.
- Supabase can store the learner's assessment scores and course progress in the same data model.
- Agent-guided lessons can become a differentiator: students are not only watching content, they are being shepherded through it.

## Feasibility

Highly feasible.

Easy:

- Course catalog.
- Lesson pages.
- Student accounts.
- Progress tracking.
- Simple quizzes and reflections.
- DesignID score-aware content blocks.

Moderate:

- Stripe enrollment and entitlement logic.
- Admin publishing workflow.
- Certificates.
- Full GHL content migration.

Advanced:

- Personalized companion agents per student design.
- Adaptive course pathways.
- Memory-aware discipleship journals.
- Cohort facilitation dashboards.

## Recommended First Win

Migrate the existing DesignID class into a custom DYDD school prototype.

The first version should prove the loop:

1. Student signs in.
2. Student sees the DesignID course.
3. Student opens a lesson.
4. Student watches/reads the content.
5. Student answers a reflection.
6. Student sees feedback personalized to their assessment pattern.

## Open Decisions

- Final project name: DYDD Online School, DYDD Academy, DesignID Academy, or Discover Your Divine Design School.
- URL/domain strategy.
- Whether this sits under the existing DYDD site or as a separate subdomain.
- How much of GHL remains in the sales funnel.
- Whether Stripe checkout lives directly in the app or through existing GHL/payment pages during the early prototype.
